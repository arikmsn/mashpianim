## 1. What the product is

Israeli UGC marketplace, Hebrew-first, RTL, mobile-first PWA. Brand name "משפיען אונליין / Mashpian", domain https://www.mashpian.online.

- **Creators** (יוצרי תוכן) get free products in exchange for a short vertical UGC video.
- **Brands** (בעלי עסקים) buy "credits" — 1 credit = 1 video — and upload products. **Until the payment provider is connected (owner decision 2026-11-10) there is no self-service purchase:** a new business starts with **0 credits**, every "רכישת סרטונים" entry shows the notice "אנא פנו אלינו בצור קשר (שבתוך האתר) לקבלת גישה." and opens the in-app contact form (`components/features/credits-access.tsx`, `lib/credits.ts`), and an admin loads the package in `/admin/brands`.
- **Matching is Tinder-style and mutual** (decided 2026-11-02):
  - A brand hearts a creator *for a specific product*; a creator hearts a product.
  - A heart is a `connections` row with `status='pending'` and `initiated_by` = `'brand'` | `'creator'`.
  - The other side hearting back (a creator's "approve", a brand's "לב בחזרה") makes the pair **mutual** (`mutual_at`, still `pending`, free) — since 2026-11-04 the match itself needs the **brand's confirmation** in its "מאטץ'" list: that is the paid step → 1 credit deducted (`match_connection()`), chat opens, two system messages are inserted (match notice + the product's brief), both sides notified in-app and via WhatsApp. A brand may also confirm a creator's heart in one step.
  - **After the match the chat drives the pipeline** (2026-11-04): the brief card (`messages.kind='brief'`, versioned snapshot in `data`) → the creator approves ("אישרתי את הבריף" in her name, `brief_approved_at`, `ship_deadline` starts) → the brand ships from the chat → "קיבלתי את החבילה" (`film_deadline` starts) → the upload is a `video` card → the brand approves or asks for one change ("דרוש שינוי: …", `max_revisions` default 1) → the revised upload closes by itself after 24 h without an answer (cron). The brand may re-post the brief at any time (new version, approval reset).
  - A creator's "✕" on a product is persistent (`creator_passes`), like the brand's "✕" on a creator.
  - **Who the business is** (owner's rule, 2026-11-09): a creator does not see which business stands behind a product until that business chose her — hearted her first, hearted her back (`mutual_at`) or confirmed the match. `brandRevealedToCreator()` in `lib/connection-flow.ts` is the one rule; `/api/products`, `/api/products/[id]` and `/api/connections` mask `brand_name` / `brand_user_id` / `logo_url` and send `brand_revealed: false` otherwise; `/api/profiles/[userId]` refuses a business profile to a creator without a revealing connection (403 `brand_hidden`). The feed card never names the business; the product page shows "עסק מאומת" with a lock until revealed. A declined heart's notification says "העסק", not the name.
  - **Chat realtime hygiene** (incident of 2026-11-09): opening a chat marked it read, the row update came back over Realtime, the chat reloaded and marked it read again — ~70 requests/s from one phone, Supabase Auth at 4–100 s, middleware killed at 25 s. Rules now: the chat marks read only when the other side wrote something newer than its read marker (`hasUnreadFromOthers`); a `connections` UPDATE that touched only quiet fields (`creator_read_at`, `brand_read_at`, `updated_at` — `QUIET_CONNECTION_FIELDS`, `isQuietConnectionUpdate` in `lib/chat.ts`) is merged locally, never refetched; the conversations list refetches on an UPDATE only when the status changed; realtime-triggered reloads are debounced (chat 350 ms, list 700 ms, nav badges 500 ms). Any new Realtime subscription must follow the same pattern: never "refetch everything on any change". Identity in the chat: `messages.sender_id` is the account's `users.id`, which is **not** the auth uid (`useAuth().user.id`) — the chat takes its own id from `/api/profile` (`selfId`); a sent message lives under a `local-` id until the saved row replaces it (`settleLocalMessage` on the POST response, `mergeIncomingMessage` on the Realtime copy).
  - A one-sided heart is answered only by the other side and withdrawn only by its sender; a mutual pair may be cancelled by either side. One-sided hearts expire after 7 days (cron); mutual pairs wait for the brand (reminder after 3 days).
  - The brand's gallery is a carousel over creators it has not hearted or skipped; "✕" is persistent (`brand_passes`).
- **5-video gate:** a creator must have ≥5 portfolio videos to heart or approve. Below that they can still *see* brand interest, but product details are blurred and buttons are locked. Enforced in the API (`lib/match.ts: countPortfolioVideos`) and in the UI.
- After a match the pipeline runs: `approved → shipped → delivered → filming → uploaded → completed` — the **brand reviews straight from `uploaded`** (approve, or `revision_requested` — the allowance comes from admin settings, default 1 since 2026-11-04); an admin may route a deliverable through the optional `in_review` step. Silence auto-approves after `review_days`; the revised (final) upload auto-completes after 24 h. Plus `rejected` / `cancelled`. The chat is open from the match until the connection closes; closed chats stay readable.
- No direct contact info between sides: chat messages pass through `lib/moderation/chat-filter.ts`.

**Storage buckets:** `videos` (private; policies `auth_users_upload_videos` + `auth_users_read_own_videos` scoped to `portfolio/{auth.uid()}/`; limit 150 MB, MIME mp4/quicktime/webm/x-m4v/3gpp), `images` (private, service role only; 5 MB, JPEG/PNG/WebP), `thumbnails` (**public**, authenticated upload to `portfolio/{auth.uid()}/`, 2 MB, images only). Videos are uploaded **directly from the browser** to Supabase Storage with a progress bar (`lib/storage-upload.ts`, XHR to the Storage REST endpoint; Vercel's 4.5 MB body limit makes server-side upload impossible), then `/api/videos/save-portfolio` or `/api/videos/save-connection` records metadata. **Plan limit:** since 2026-11-10 the project is on Supabase Pro and the project-wide upload limit (Dashboard → Storage → Settings) is 150 MB, so the cap users see is **150 MB** (`MAX_VIDEO_MB` in `lib/uploads.ts`, ≈ 2 minutes of 1080p; the oversize message tells them to shorten the clip or drop to 720p). `MAX_VIDEO_MB` must never exceed the project-wide limit — a 51 MB probe upload is the quick way to check it.

**Branding assets:** `public/logo.png` is the single source (transparent rounded-square gradient with the white phone/M artwork). Every icon is generated from it by `scripts/gen-icons.py` (Pillow + numpy): `icon-192/512.png` (manifest `any`, transparent corners), `icon-192/512-maskable.png` (manifest `maskable`: gradient bleeds past the canvas, artwork inside the 40 % safe zone so circle/squircle launchers don't clip it), `apple-icon.png` (iOS, opaque) and `favicon.ico`. After replacing `logo.png`: run the script, then bump `CACHE_NAME`/`STATIC_CACHE` in `public/sw.js` — the service worker caches images, and installed apps keep the old files until a new worker activates (images are also refreshed in the background on every visit since v3). `manifest.json` `background_color`/`theme_color` are the brand pink `#E1306C` (splash screen and status bar); pages, the install prompt and the offline page show `logo.png` directly.

## 6. Front-end conventions that matter

- Pages are client components. Data loading uses `swrFetch(url, apply)` from `lib/client-cache.ts`: `apply` runs with the cached response immediately (instant revisit) and again with fresh data. Mutations refetch via `fetchJson`. Supabase Realtime (`postgres_changes`) is used on `connections`, `products`, `messages` to refresh lists.
- Auth: `useAuth()` is a single shared store; `(app)/layout.tsx` redirects to `/login?redirect=` when there is no session. Role is fetched from `/api/profile` (cached) — BottomNav shows creator or brand tabs accordingly; the profile, notifications, contact and logout live in the side menu (☰ in the bar, `SideMenu` rendered by the layout).
- UI primitives live in `components/ui`; `Avatar` has `expandable` (lightbox); `ProfileModal userId=…` shows any user's public profile anywhere.
- All user-facing text is Hebrew. JSX text containing `'` must use `&apos;` (eslint `react/no-unescaped-entities`).
- Lint state: `npx tsc --noEmit` is clean and must stay clean. ESLint has ~15 **pre-existing** errors (`react-hooks` "setState synchronously in effect", "cannot access variable before declared") and widespread `no-explicit-any` — the codebase uses `as any` for Supabase rows. Don't add new errors; don't feel obliged to fix old ones unless touching that code.
- Unread badge: the messages page emits `emitUnreadChanged(delta)` (window event) and BottomNav listens; it also refetches on visibility change.
- Video performance: never render `<video>` in lists; use `thumbnail_url` posters (`preload="none"`) and only autoplay the single focused card. Videos without a poster use `src="…#t=0.1"` so Safari paints a frame.
